question: 'Are the sessions live or recorded?'
sort_order: 2
---

Lectures are pre-recorded and available in the YouTube playlist. New workshops or updated videos are announced on Slack and Telegram.

Live sessions are primarily for Q&A and are announced separately - there is no regular schedule for them. When a live session takes place, the recording is posted on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DataTalksClub).
