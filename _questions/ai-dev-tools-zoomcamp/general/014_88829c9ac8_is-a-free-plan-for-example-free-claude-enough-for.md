question: Is a free plan (for example, free Claude) enough for the course, or do I
  need a paid subscription?
sort_order: 14
---

Most likely the free plan won't be enough. Plan to spend at least $10 on a paid
plan from a provider of your choice.

A $10 plan, such as OpenCode Go, should be fine. Be careful in Module 1: long
`/goal` runs over a whole website can drain the quota. Test the workflow on a
small scope until you understand the concept, then use it as usual in the later
modules.
