question: Can I check my homework answers after the deadline? Is there a reference
  solution?
sort_order: 16
---

You can still do the homework after the deadline to test yourself, but you can't
submit it once the form is closed.

There's no reference solution, but after the homework is scored, the
[course management platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/ai-dev-tools-2026/)
shows the correct answers, so compare yours with them.
