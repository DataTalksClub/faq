question: How many points do I need on the final project to pass, and what else is
  required to earn the certificate?
sort_order: 18
---

To pass the final project for the current ML Zoomcamp cohort, you need at least 7 points.

To confirm the latest requirement for your cohort (since grading can change), check your course dashboard under the “Project outcomes” section. Note that the dashboard may not be available right at the beginning of the course.

Also, passing the project score is not the only requirement for the certificate: you must complete all your assigned peer reviews to qualify for the certificate, regardless of your project score.
