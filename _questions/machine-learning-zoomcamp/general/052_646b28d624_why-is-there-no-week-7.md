Week 7 is the midterm project. There are no lectures for it, so there is no `07-*` folder in the course repo and no Week 7 in the video playlist. The repo goes straight from `06-trees` to `08-deep-learning`.

You spend that week building and submitting the [midterm project](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/machine-learning-zoomcamp/tree/master/projects#midterm-project) instead. See the project guidelines for requirements and the deadline.
