- I can write a recommendation letter if I can honestly endorse your work from the ML Zoomcamp 2025 and related coursework. If I don’t feel comfortable making a strong endorsement, I’ll be honest and suggest alternatives.
- What I’d need from you: a current resume; the job posting or description; the deadline; to whom the letter should be addressed; any specific points you want highlighted (e.g., projects, skills, teamwork); preferred tone (formal or concise); whether you want a short (about 200–300 words) or longer letter; and a few bullet points I can reference.
- What I can provide: a draft letter or notes, a bullet-point reference sheet, and a tailored letter that reflects your strengths and the job requirements; I will not make statements I can’t substantiate.
- Suggested process: 1) share materials and deadline; 2) I confirm I can provide a solid endorsement; 3) I draft the letter and share it with you; 4) you review and we revise as needed; 5) you use the final version for your applications.
- Template you can send to the recommender (you can customize):

  Subject: Request for Letter of Recommendation
  Dear [Name],
  I am applying for [Job Title] at [Company]. I would be grateful if you could write a letter of recommendation highlighting my work in the ML Zoomcamp 2025, especially [skills/achievements]. The deadline is [Date]. I can share my resume and a brief note with suggested points. If helpful, I can draft a version for your review.
  Thank you for considering this request.
  Best regards,
  [Your Name]

  To whom it may concern,
  I am pleased to recommend [Student] who completed the ML Zoomcamp 2025 under my supervision. [Student] demonstrated strong abilities in [areas], delivering [projects] with [outcomes]. They showed initiative, critical thinking, and effective collaboration. I believe they would be an excellent candidate for [position] at [Company].

  [Recommender Name], [Title] – [Organization]
