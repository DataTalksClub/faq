In the most recent cohort's dashboard, 766 of the 885 evaluated projects passed (about 87%) and 119 failed.

The "Project completion rate" in the left panel (18.7%) is a different number. It's the share of all enrolled students who submitted a project, and many people register without ever submitting. That's why it's much lower than the pass rate.
