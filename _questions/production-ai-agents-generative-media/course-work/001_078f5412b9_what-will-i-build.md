A Personalized Media Factory. A brief goes in: a task prompt, a data record and constraints (format, style, budget). An agent plans the asset and writes its text, GPU models generate an image plus voice or video, a check regenerates the asset when it isn't good enough, and the factory scales from 1 to 1,000+ records with a cost per asset. The worked example is the Santa Christmas Challenge.
