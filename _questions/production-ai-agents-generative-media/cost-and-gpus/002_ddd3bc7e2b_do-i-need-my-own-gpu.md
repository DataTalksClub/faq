question: 'Do I need my own GPU, and who pays for GPU usage?'
sort_order: 2
---

You do not need your own GPU or cluster: the models run as Jobs and Endpoints on Nebius Serverless AI. Applicants get GPU credits through the [Nebius AI Builder Program](https://dev.nebius.com/builders), which is free to join. Usage beyond the credits is billed to your Nebius account.

Each module reports the cost per asset, so you know what a run costs before you scale it. If you follow along without applying, you need your own GPU access and pay for your own usage.
