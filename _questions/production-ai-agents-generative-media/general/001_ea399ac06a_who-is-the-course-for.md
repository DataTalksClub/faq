question: 'Who is this course for and what do I need to know before I start?'
sort_order: 1

The course is for working builders running open or custom GPU media models: AI/ML engineers adding image, video or audio generation to real systems, MLOps and ML platform engineers who own the batch pipeline and the cost line, and technical founders paying per-call media APIs who want to know when self-hosting wins.

You need Python, Docker basics, HTTP APIs and the command line. Prior LLM or MLOps course experience helps but isn't required. The level is intermediate to advanced.

The course does not cover diffusion maths, training foundation models, deep fine-tuning, CUDA, Kubernetes, RAG or LLM fundamentals.
