question: I submitted my capstone project. What are the remaining certificate requirements,
  and when do I get my score and feedback?
sort_order: 27
---

Submitting the project is not the last step. To meet the certificate
requirements you also have to complete the peer reviews you are assigned, within
the peer-review window.

You don't need to request anything or sign up for them. Once project submissions
close, your review assignments appear automatically on the
[course management platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/llm-zoomcamp-2026/),
together with the peer-review deadline. You can't choose which projects you get.

Your own score and the reviewers' feedback become visible only after the
peer-review deadline passes. An empty score before then is normal, and it is not
a reason to submit the project again as attempt 2 - resubmitting starts a fresh
round and gives you another set of projects to review. Submit again only if you
actually want to improve the project.
