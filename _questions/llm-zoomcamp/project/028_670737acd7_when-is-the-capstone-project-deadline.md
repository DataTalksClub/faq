The submission dates for the running cohort are on the
[course management platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/llm-zoomcamp-2026/),
next to the submission form.

Check them there rather than on a past cohort's page: each cohort sets its own
dates for attempt 1 and attempt 2, and they move from cohort to cohort.
