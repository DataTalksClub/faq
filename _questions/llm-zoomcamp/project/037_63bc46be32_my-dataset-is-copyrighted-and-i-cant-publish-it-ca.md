question: My dataset is copyrighted and I can't publish it. Can I still use it for
  the project?
sort_order: 37
---

Yes, but reviewers won't be able to reproduce your project without the data. To
avoid that, generate synthetic documents that look similar to yours and publish
those instead. Synthetic data is fine for the project.

If you don't, you lose the reproducibility points. That isn't the end of the
world, and you can still pass the project.
