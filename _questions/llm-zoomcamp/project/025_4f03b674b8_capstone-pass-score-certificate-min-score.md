question: What minimum score is required to pass the capstone project and qualify
  for the certificate?
sort_order: 25
---

For the current LLM Zoomcamp cohort, the minimum score required to pass the capstone project is 11 points.

You can check the current pass mark on the course dashboard under “Project outcomes.” This requirement may change for future cohorts, so always refer to your cohort’s dashboard for the most up-to-date information.

Your final project score is the median of the scores given by three assigned peer reviewers. To qualify for the certificate, you must also complete the required peer reviews of other students’ projects.
