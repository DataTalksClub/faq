question: I passed the project. When will I get my certificate?
sort_order: 32
---

You'll get it after the last project attempt and its peer review are over.
Certificates are generated manually, in one batch for all graduates of the
cohort.

So if you passed an earlier attempt, wait until the remaining attempts are
finished. You don't need to request the certificate. Before then, make sure the
certificate name in your profile on the
[course management platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/llm-zoomcamp-2026/)
is the one you want printed.

When your certificate is ready, you get an email. If you don't see it, check your
spam folder, and mark the email as "not spam" if it's there.
