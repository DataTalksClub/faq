question: How long do I have to complete the peer reviews once they're assigned?
sort_order: 24
---

About a week. Peer review opens after the project submission form closes, and
the review window runs roughly one week from there.

The exact deadline is shown on the course management platform alongside your
assigned projects — check it there rather than counting days yourself, since the
cohort lead opens reviews manually and the date can shift.

Reviews only count while the window is open. If you miss it, the submission
doesn't count toward your certificate, even if the project itself passed.
