question: When and how will we be assigned projects for review/grading?
sort_order: 5
---

After project submissions close, your peer-review assignments become available
on the course-management platform. Use the platform to see the projects assigned
to you and the separate peer-review deadline.

You get the assignments automatically - there is no sign-up step, and you don't
need to ask the course team for them. You also can't pick which projects you
review.
